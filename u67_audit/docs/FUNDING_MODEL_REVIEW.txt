U67 FUNDING-MODEL REVIEW (Stage 4, item 4) - 2026-10-30
Scope: both engines (S and L) and the S3 decision scenario. Frozen rules and thresholds NOT changed.

FINDINGS (verified in both engine sources, S3 cost block):
F1. Funding term = S3_FUNDING_PER_8H (0.00010) x bars_held x 0.5, bars_held = number of 4H bars. It is an AVERAGE flat rate, not real cash flows.
F2. NOT charged at actual funding timestamps (00/08/16 UTC). Real funding events inside a holding = floor(n/2)..ceil(n/2) of n bars, depending on entry candle.
F3. NO sign handling. Funding is charged as a COST in every case, for both S and L. Reality: positive funding is PAID by longs and RECEIVED by shorts; negative funding is the opposite.
    - U67-S: flat charge is conservative when funding is positive (normal), understated when funding is negative (squeeze) or when the rate is higher than 0.01%/8h.
    - U67-L: flat charge matches the direction of normal positive funding but UNDERSTATES it whenever real funding > 0.01%/8h (hype / new listings can be several times higher). Funding negative = longs receive (flat charge conservative).
F4. Real funding rates are not measured (S4 needs designer authorization; unchanged).
