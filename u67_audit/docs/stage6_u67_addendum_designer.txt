3. FUNDING BOUNDARY AND EVENT-COUNT AUDIT (analysis only; no engine/evaluator/registration change)
Official sources read: KuCoin Support "Funding Fees" (kucoin.com/support/26686295987353) and the announcement "Adjustment to Perpetual Contract Funding Fee Settlement Times and Payment Rules".
VERIFIED: (i) only users who HOLD an open position at the settlement time pay/receive; positions fully closed before settlement pay nothing. (ii) Funding fee = position value x funding rate, position value at the MARK price at settlement time (not entry notional). (iii) Major pairs settle every 8h; most other pairs every 4h; cycle may shorten to 1h in strong premium/discount and revert to 4h (page: "actual times may change", real-time times on the trading page). (iv) For 8h-cycle contracts the announcement gives settlement at 00:00/08:00/16:00 UTC (rollout 2025-06). (v) The automatic hourly/4-hour mechanism (announcement dated 2026-08-17, effective after 2026-08-17 08:00 UTC) applies to all USDT/USDC perpetuals.
NOT VERIFIABLE from official text: eligibility of a position opened exactly at the timestamp (the page only says a position opened at 00:00:20 "could still be subject" because assessment can take up to a minute) and of a position closed exactly at the timestamp. These boundary treatments are UNRESOLVED.
IMPORTANT CORRECTION to Stage 5 Part 2 B and D: that part assumed 8-hourly events for all contracts. Per the official page most non-major contracts settle every 4h, so the "15 events" figure holds ONLY for an 8h-cycle contract and only under the convention entry-inclusive/exit-exclusive. Enumeration for a 30-bar window (120 h):
 interval | entry hour (UTC) | in/in | in/ex | ex/in | ex/ex   (in=timestamp counts, ex=does not; entry/exit)
 8h  | 00, 08 or 16 (aligned)   | 16 | 15 | 15 | 14
 8h  | 04, 12 or 20 (unaligned) | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15
 4h  | any 4H candle open       | 31 | 30 | 30 | 29
 1h  | any 4H candle open       | 121 | 120 | 120 | 119
So the event count of a 30-bar trade is 14-16 (8h), 29-31 (4h) or 119-121 (1h); a trade closed by SL/TP inside a bar pays only for settlements before the exit instant. Effect of one event on R (30 bars, per-event rate r): at risk 1.5%: r=0.10% -> 0.067R, r=0.05% (4h equivalent of 0.10%/8h) -> 0.033R; at risk 3%: half of those. Stage 5 arithmetic (events x r / risk) remains valid; the event counts are boundary-uncertain, the sign logic (long pays / short receives when funding > 0, reversed when < 0) is unchanged. Event times cannot be inferred from candle counts.

4. INTERVAL MECHANISM (limitation, confirmed from the official announcement)
Automatic move to hourly settlement when a contract's funding rate hits its upper/lower limit; restoration to 4h after 16 consecutive hourly periods with |rate| <= 0.025%. 00:00/08:00/16:00 UTC are NOT guaranteed timestamps for every contract; the interval is contract-specific and time-varying. Any measured funding scenario (S4, only if ever authorized) must record per contract: actual settlement timestamp, interval, rate, and position eligibility (held at the settlement time), with mark-price position value.
