2. Resolve the four V2 specification decisions before implementation

(a) Primary treatment of censored trades

Registrar proposal:

- A trade with no symbol candle for 45 days is unresolved/censored, not assumed to have hit its stop.
- It remains assigned to its original cluster but contributes no R to the primary cluster mean.
- Cluster mean is calculated from resolved trades only.
- A cluster with zero resolved trades is labelled CENSORED and excluded from primary N.
- The checkpoint condition becomes “no open trades except censored trades.”

Designer must confirm or amend this complete treatment. Specify exactly how censored clusters are counted in every denominator, minimum-cluster threshold, bootstrap draw, and checkpoint calculation. Do not silently remove censored observations.

(b) Bias guard

The proposed -1R sensitivity is a fixed stress assumption, not necessarily a conservative loss bound. A delisted or halted LONG position may lose more than 1R.

Require the designer to decide:

- Whether an additional harsher stress scenario is required for delisted/ halted positions, or whether -1R is explicitly accepted only as a limited fixed stress test.

Do not describe -1R as a guaranteed worst-case or conservative outcome.
