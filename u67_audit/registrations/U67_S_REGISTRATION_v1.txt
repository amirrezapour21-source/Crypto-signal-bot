0. LEAKAGE AND DISCLOSURE STATEMENT
a) NOT done by the registrar: no historical simulation, no signal count, no outcome, no plot or table of any C11-rule result on the 67 symbols. Only market metadata (24h turnover, contract fields, completed-candle counts and contiguity) was read to define the universe.
b) Inherited and disclosed: every strategy parameter equals C11's and was originally chosen on the 33-symbol universe with SHORT results on historical data (backtest, holdout, forward). U67-S therefore tests generalization of a frozen rule to different symbols; it carries NO tuning on the new symbols.
c) Prior exposure of the new symbols to OTHER studies (different strategies/purposes; the registrar did not run the C11 rule on them): 13 of the 67 appear in the MicroMAP snapshot/data-diagnostic workflows (microstructure data pulls), 48 appear in a V4-SMC metadata audit, 11 in the old V3 bot signals log (signals_log.json); a few one-letter matches (S, MET, H) in other files may be false matches. Counts are by text match of the symbol strings in repository files (heuristic; one-letter symbols may give false matches). Exposure of the 67 symbols to those studies was never an outcome exposure of the C11 SHORT rule by the registrar. If the designer knows of any C11-rule SHORT result previously seen on any of the 67 symbols, he may state it as an addendum; an addendum does not alter any rule.
d) Selection timing (explicit): the contracts snapshot was taken 2026-10-28T05:26:56.667Z-05:26:57.612Z, i.e. BEFORE this registration's freeze, by a one-shot read-only GitHub Actions run. The exclusion of non-crypto-underlying contracts was decided AFTER inspecting the selection metadata (symbol names and turnover), by the designer's ruling. No outcome data of any kind was retrieved or used at any step.
e) Evaluation uses only forward data: trades whose entry time is at or after the later of (i) 2026-10-28T12:00:00Z and (ii) the engine state's created_at. Earlier signals are never backfilled or replayed.

1. HYPOTHESIS (one, no alternatives)
On 4H candles of the 67 KuCoin USDT-perpetual crypto symbols in section 2, when price is in a downtrend (close below EMA200), has just made a statistically extreme upward close (z-score of close over 20 bars >= +2) and the first completed candle returns below +2 on above-median range and above-median volume, a SHORT entered at the next candle open with stop 1.25 ATR20 and target 2R, held at most 30 candles, has positive expectancy after the S3 cost scenario. This is the exact C11 hypothesis applied to a disjoint symbol set. Disjoint means no shared symbols; it does NOT mean independent in time or market regime: U67-S runs in the same calendar window as C11 and LONG-M1 and altcoins share BTC beta. Falsifiable by section 8.

Universe integrity: no symbol is ever added, removed or replaced; eligibility is re-tested by the engine every run and a symbol that later fails is not edited out. A symbol that is delisted, halted or persistently erroring is reported (counts only) and produces no signals; the verdict stands on the symbols that remain available. Engine behavior stated precisely: a symbol whose last 250 completed candles contain a gap fails that run; its cursor (last_ts) does not advance, so monitoring catches up when data is clean again, and any catch-up signal on a candle processed more than 90 minutes after its entry time is recorded MISSED. Signals on candles before the last completed candle of a symbol's first successful run are not recorded at all (no replay, as in C11).
UNRESOLVED positions (evaluation-time rule, mechanical, price-independent; NO engine change): at the evaluation instant a position that is still in the state's open set is declared UNRESOLVED if the instant minus the cursor symbols[<symbol>].last_ts of its symbol (last successfully processed candle) is at least 45 days. 45 days exceeds the engine's own 250-bar (about 41.7 days) contiguity-recovery window and the 5-day maximum holding. Status is fixed at the evaluation instant: a position closed by the engine before that instant keeps its engine outcome; after the instant the fixed checkpoint is never recomputed. An UNRESOLVED position is counted conservatively as a stop-out in EVERY scenario (S0, S1, S2, S3, S3a, S3b): gross R = -1, exit price = stop, bars_held = 30, each scenario's own cost formula with the stored entry, risk and stop. It is flagged in the report and a sensitivity excluding it is shown. Fields used: open[].symbol/entry/risk/sl/signal_ts and symbols[].last_ts, all already stored by the engine. The rule is computed by the evaluator, never written into the engine state.
Run health (engine rule): a run is DEGRADED when MORE than 20% of the 67 symbols fail in that run, i.e. 14 or more (13/67 = 19.4% is not degraded; 14/67 = 20.9% is). A degraded run saves state, skips the report and exits non-zero (the workflow then fails and alerts). Degradation is operational only: it voids no trade, no cluster and no checkpoint.
State continuity: the git history of u67_short_paper_state.json is the record. If the state file is ever lost or recreated, it must be restored from the last committed version; a silently recreated state (the engine creates a new one with a later eligible_from) is a defect, not a restart, and the affected period is labeled. Eligibility compares the trade's entry_ts, not the signal time, with eligible_from.
Log retention: the engine keeps bounded lists (errors 100, missed 500, signals 500, closed 5000). Full history is preserved in git (every state change is committed). Closed trades are never truncated before UC2 (expected volume is far below 5000).
Attrition of the universe is reported in the registrar's daily counts-only review (a review practice, not an engine feature).

3. INDICATORS (identical to C11 prepare())
ATR20 = EWM(alpha=1/20, adjust=False) of true range. z20 = (close - SMA20(close)) / STD20(close, ddof=1); std 0 -> NaN. EMA200 = close EWM(span=200, adjust=False). range20 = rolling max high 20 - rolling min low 20; range_median20 = rolling median 20 of range20. vol_median20 = rolling median 20 of volume. Any NaN -> no signal.

4. SIGNAL (candle t completed, all must hold)
(1) z20[t-1] >= +2.0 and z20[t] < +2.0
(2) close[t] < EMA200[t]
(3) range20[t] >= range_median20[t]
(4) volume[t] >= vol_median20[t]
(5) ATR20[t] finite and > 0

5. TRADE RULES (identical to C11)
Entry: open of candle t+1 (entry_ts = signal_ts + 4h). If the engine sees it more than 90 minutes after entry_ts: MISSED (ENTRY_DELAY_EXCEEDED), never backfilled, never reclassified. Entry candle missing/invalid: MISSED. MIN_CANDLES = 250.
Risk = 1.25 x ATR20[t]; stop = entry + risk; target = entry - 2 x risk; risk_pct = risk/entry. No risk_pct filter.
One open position per symbol; a second signal while open is recorded MISSED (SYMBOL_ALREADY_OPEN). No portfolio cap; maximum concurrent open trades is reported.
Monitoring (entry candle counts as bar 1): per candle, stop first (high >= stop -> gross R = -1), then target (low <= target -> gross R = +2), else at bar 30 exit at that candle's close, gross R = (entry - close)/risk. Stop fills at the stop level and target at the target level (paper model; optimistic in gaps; see stop_gap). exit_ts = open time of the exit candle; bars_held counts entry and exit candles. stop_gap = (exit_reason == SL and the exit candle's open >= stop); recorded only, never alters the outcome.
No parameter, filter, symbol or rule changes during the test (section 9).

6. COSTS AND NET R
net R = gross R - cost_R; all costs are fractions of entry notional; cost_R = total fraction / risk_pct.
S0 (state field net_r): flat 0.20% round trip, no funding.
S3 (DECISION scenario, state field net_r_s3): fee 0.15% per side: 0.0015 + 0.0015 x (exit_price/entry); funding assumed 0.010% per 8h charged as a cost on entry notional: 0.00010 x bars_held x 0.5 (upper bound: holding = bars_held x 4h); no slippage. The funding is a flat assumption, labeled "assumed", and is NOT a bound: for hype or newly listed symbols real funding can be several times larger, and shorts pay when funding turns negative after a squeeze. For shorts positive funding is normally received, so charging it is conservative in ordinary conditions; it is charged as a cost in every case. Real funding is not measured (S4 needs the designer's authorization).
S3a / S3b (sensitivities only): S3 with 0.00025 x (1 + exit_price/entry) / 0.0005 x (1 + exit_price/entry) added to the total cost fraction (0.05% / 0.10% round trip, split equally between entry and exit). Smaller symbols may have higher real slippage; S3 contains none by the designer's ruling. S3a/S3b are reported on every checkpoint; if S3 passes while S3a or S3b does not, that is reported descriptively and changes no verdict.
S1: all-market fee 0.0006 x (1 + exit_price/entry), no funding. S2: S1, except trades with exit_reason == TP use 0.0004 x (1 + exit_price/entry). No funding. S1/S2 transparency only.
S4 (measured funding) only if the designer authorizes funding-history access. Paper results are not proof of real execution or profitability.

7. CLUSTERS, CHECKPOINTS, STATISTICS (as LONG-M1 section 7, adapted)
Cluster = all eligible trades sharing the same signal-candle timestamp (strict count; primary). Sensitivity: adjacent consecutive 4H signal candles merged. Cluster outcome = equal-weight mean net R of its closed trades; the cluster-level mean (equal weight per cluster) is the primary statistic. Eligible trades only (section 0e); MISSED entries are reported separately, not counted, not backfilled.
Cluster formation: a cluster contains only OPENED eligible trades with the same signal-candle timestamp; MISSED signals never create, join or alter a cluster. All trades of one signal candle are opened within 90 minutes after its entry time (later processing is MISSED), so a cluster is complete 90 minutes after its entry_ts. Clusters are ordered by signal_ts.
Selection of clusters (no cherry-picking): UC1 uses the FIRST 15 strict clusters ordered by signal_ts; UC2 uses the FIRST 30 (cumulative, includes the UC1 clusters). A checkpoint is evaluated exactly once, on the FIRST committed state in the repository's git history in which the first N clusters (N=15 for UC1, 30 for UC2) all exist, are complete and have zero open trades (every trade closed by TP, SL or 30-bar timeout, or counted as UNRESOLVED relative to that commit's time). The evaluation instant is the commit time of that state, found by the evaluator scanning the history from the first state commit onward; the registrar's review time is irrelevant and he has no discretion over it. The trigger is detected from counts only; no R value is viewed before it holds. The cluster-timestamp set is then fixed; degraded runs do not matter because evaluation reads committed states, not runs. The evaluator (cluster building, bootstrap, jackknife, UNRESOLVED rule, and a check that the state's created_at equals that of the first committed state; a mismatch labels the period as a defect) is a single script committed to the repo, tested on synthetic data only, and its sha256 is recorded in the repository before 5 clusters exist; any later change to it follows section 9. Later clusters are excluded from that checkpoint and its statistics even if they close earlier. Open trades outside those clusters are reported, not counted.
No outcome review before UC1; operational status (counts of signals, open, closed, missed, errors, symbol health) may be reported earlier, R values are not reported or acted upon earlier.
Statistics (fixed): cluster-mean net R by S0, S3, S3a, S3b (S1, S2 labeled, transparency only); 95% CI = percentile 2.5/97.5 of the mean of cluster means, resampling strict clusters with replacement, 20000 draws, numpy default_rng seed 20261028; P(mean>0) descriptive only. Also: trade-level mean, profit factor ("n/a" if no losses), max drawdown in R (trades ordered by exit_ts, ties by signal_ts then symbol), maximum concurrent open trades; breakdown by calendar month (month of signal_ts, UTC) and by symbol; BTC change over the window as one descriptive label (no filter).
Jackknife definitions: largest cluster = the cluster with the highest S3 cluster-mean net R, i.e. the largest contributor to the primary statistic (ties to the earlier signal_ts). Note: LONG-M1 section 7 wording ("highest S3 net R sum") is ambiguous between a trade-level sum and the cluster-mean; for U67-S the cluster-mean reading is registered; best month = the calendar month (signal_ts UTC) with the highest S3 net R sum of cluster outcomes (ties to the earlier). Each is removed once; the remaining statistic is the equal-weight mean of the remaining cluster means. If a removal leaves zero clusters, that jackknife mean is treated as <= 0.
Rate cap: if the UC1 condition (section 7, first 15 clusters complete and fully closed) is not met 120 days after the section 0e eligibility boundary, or the UC2 condition is not met 240 days after it, the observed rate is reported; no verdict. The only permitted actions are (a) continue unchanged or (b) stop the track; the choice is made once per cap, recorded before any R value is viewed, and rules, symbols and parameters are never altered.

8. DECISION RULES (decided now; S3 is the sole decision scenario)
- UC1 (15 clusters): only the rejection rule is binding. If S3 cluster-mean <= 0: REJECTED, the track is stopped; the designer may only archive it or register a new hypothesis (new registration, section 9); rules are not altered and not retested on the same observations. If S3 cluster-mean > 0: continue to UC2 with no verdict; CI and jackknife are descriptive at UC1.
- UC2 (30 clusters): REJECTED as above if S3 cluster-mean <= 0. NOT ESTABLISHED if S3 mean > 0 but CI95 lower bound <= 0, or removing the largest cluster or the best month leaves a mean <= 0. NOT ESTABLISHED is final for U67-S v1: no extension, no additional clusters, no re-evaluation; any further data requires a new registration. PAPER GATE PASSED only if S3 cluster-mean > 0 AND CI95 lower bound > 0 AND mean without the largest cluster > 0 AND mean without the best month > 0. The paper gate authorizes nothing but a further review; a real-money pilot always needs a separate explicit authorization per track and per direction.
- Tracks are judged on their own. U67-S results are never combined with C11, LONG-M1 or any LONG track; opposite positions on the same symbol in different tracks are not netted. Symbols are disjoint between U67-S and C11/LONG-M1 by construction.
Compatibility note (documented before freeze, as the designer allowed): the C11 protocol treats a non-positive S3 cluster-mean at a checkpoint as a "rejection-review flag" (rules unaltered, designer reviews), while this registration adopts the binding REJECTED wording of the designer-approved, independently audited LONG-M1 registration. This changes the consequence at UC1, not the statistic. It is a registrar choice to be confirmed at the readiness review: if the designer prefers the C11 review-flag semantics he must say so BEFORE deployment, and the registration will be re-frozen as v2 with its own freeze and eligibility literals and a new hash (no outcome data can exist before deployment).
Limits of inference (stated now): clusters are treated as exchangeable in the bootstrap, but adjacent 4H clusters are dependent (the adjacent-merge sensitivity shows this); the confidence interval is conditional on that assumption. The 15-cluster rejection test and the 30-cluster test reuse the first 15 clusters, so no multiplicity correction is made. The universe is the top of one day's turnover ranking, which favors currently active names.

9. CHANGE CONTROL
Any change to rules, parameters, symbols or classification = a new registration with a new counter; old evidence is kept, labeled, never pooled. Code defects are fixed only to restore registered behavior; each fix is documented with its commit and the affected period labeled. No tuning of any kind on observed results.
