Universe integrity: no symbol is ever added, removed or replaced; eligibility is re-tested by the engine every run and a symbol that later fails is not edited out. A symbol that is delisted, halted or persistently erroring is reported (counts only) and produces no signals; the verdict stands on the symbols that remain available. Engine behavior stated precisely: a symbol whose last 250 completed candles contain a gap fails that run; its cursor (last_ts) does not advance, so monitoring catches up when data is clean again, and any catch-up signal on a candle processed more than 90 minutes after its entry time is recorded MISSED. Signals on candles before the last completed candle of a symbol's first successful run are not recorded at all (no replay, as in C11).
CENSORED positions (evaluation-time rule, v2; replaces the v1 UNRESOLVED stop-out rule, which is withdrawn for v2; mechanical, price-independent; NO engine change): at the evaluation instant a position still in the state's open set is CENSORED if the instant minus symbols[<symbol>].last_ts (last successfully processed candle of its symbol) is at least 45 days. 45 days exceeds the engine's 250-bar (about 41.7 days) contiguity-recovery window and the 5-day maximum holding. Status is fixed at the evaluation instant and never recomputed; a position closed by the engine before that instant keeps its engine outcome. A censored trade stays in its cluster and carries NO R in the primary analysis (section 7A). Fields used: open[].symbol/entry/risk/sl/signal_ts and symbols[].last_ts, all already stored by the engine. The rule is computed by the evaluator, never written into the engine state.
Run health (engine rule): a run is DEGRADED when MORE than 20% of the 67 symbols fail in that run, i.e. 14 or more (13/67 = 19.4% is not degraded; 14/67 = 20.9% is). A degraded run saves state, skips the report and exits non-zero (the workflow then fails and alerts). Degradation is operational only: it voids no trade, no cluster and no checkpoint.
State continuity: the git history of u67_long_paper_state.json is the record. If the state file is ever lost or recreated, it must be restored from the last committed version; a silently recreated state (the engine creates a new one with a later eligible_from) is a defect, not a restart, and the affected period is labeled. Eligibility compares the trade's entry_ts, not the signal time, with eligible_from.
Log retention: the engine keeps bounded lists (errors 100, missed 500, signals 500, closed 5000). Full history is preserved in git (every state change is committed). Closed trades are never truncated before UL2 (expected volume is far below 5000).
Attrition of the universe is reported in the registrar's daily counts-only review (a review practice, not an engine feature).

3. INDICATORS (identical to C11 prepare())
ATR20 = EWM(alpha=1/20, adjust=False) of true range. z20 = (close - SMA20(close)) / STD20(close, ddof=1); std 0 -> NaN. EMA200 = close EWM(span=200, adjust=False). range20 = rolling max high 20 - rolling min low 20; range_median20 = rolling median 20 of range20. vol_median20 = rolling median 20 of volume. Any NaN -> no signal.

4. SIGNAL (candle t completed, all must hold)
(1) z20[t-1] <= -2.0 and z20[t] > -2.0
(2) close[t] > EMA200[t]
(3) range20[t] >= range_median20[t]
(4) volume[t] >= vol_median20[t]
(5) ATR20[t] finite and > 0

5. TRADE RULES (identical to LONG-M1)
Entry: open of candle t+1 (entry_ts = signal_ts + 4h). If the engine sees it more than 90 minutes after entry_ts: MISSED (ENTRY_DELAY_EXCEEDED), never backfilled, never reclassified. Entry candle missing/invalid: MISSED. MIN_CANDLES = 250.
Risk = 1.25 x ATR20[t]; stop = entry - risk; target = entry + 2 x risk; risk_pct = risk/entry. No risk_pct filter.
One open position per symbol; a second signal while open is recorded MISSED (SYMBOL_ALREADY_OPEN). No portfolio cap; maximum concurrent open trades is reported.
Monitoring (entry candle counts as bar 1): per candle, stop first (low <= stop -> gross R = -1), then target (high >= target -> gross R = +2), else at bar 30 exit at that candle's close, gross R = (close - entry)/risk. Stop fills at the stop level and target at the target level (paper model; optimistic in gaps; see stop_gap). exit_ts = open time of the exit candle; bars_held counts entry and exit candles. stop_gap = (exit_reason == SL and the exit candle's open <= stop); recorded only, never alters the outcome.
No parameter, filter, symbol or rule changes during the test (section 9).

6. COSTS AND NET R
net R = gross R - cost_R; all costs are fractions of entry notional; cost_R = total fraction / risk_pct.
S0 (state field net_r): flat 0.20% round trip, no funding.
S3 (DECISION scenario, state field net_r_s3): fee 0.15% per side: 0.0015 + 0.0015 x (exit_price/entry); funding assumed 0.010% per 8h charged as a cost on entry notional: 0.00010 x bars_held x 0.5 (upper bound: holding = bars_held x 4h); no slippage. The funding is a flat assumption, labeled "assumed", and is NOT a bound: for hype or newly listed symbols real funding can be several times larger, and longs can pay several times more during crowded rallies. For longs positive funding is normally paid, so the assumed charge is realistic in ordinary conditions and understated when funding is high; it is charged as a cost in every case (conservative only when funding is negative). Real funding is not measured (S4 needs the designer's authorization).
S3a / S3b (sensitivities only): S3 with 0.00025 x (1 + exit_price/entry) / 0.0005 x (1 + exit_price/entry) added to the total cost fraction (0.05% / 0.10% round trip, split equally between entry and exit). Smaller symbols may have higher real slippage; S3 contains none by the designer's ruling. S3a/S3b are reported on every checkpoint; if S3 passes while S3a or S3b does not, that is reported descriptively and changes no verdict.
S1: all-market fee 0.0006 x (1 + exit_price/entry), no funding. S2: S1, except trades with exit_reason == TP use 0.0004 x (1 + exit_price/entry). No funding. S1/S2 transparency only.
S4 (measured funding) only if the designer authorizes funding-history access. Paper results are not proof of real execution or profitability.

7. CLUSTERS, CHECKPOINTS, STATISTICS (as LONG-M1 section 7, adapted)
Cluster = all eligible trades sharing the same signal-candle timestamp (strict count; primary). Sensitivity: adjacent consecutive 4H signal candles merged. Cluster outcome = equal-weight mean net R of its closed trades; the cluster-level mean (equal weight per cluster) is the primary statistic. Eligible trades only (section 0e); MISSED entries are reported separately, not counted, not backfilled.
Cluster formation: a cluster contains only OPENED eligible trades with the same signal-candle timestamp; MISSED signals never create, join or alter a cluster. All trades of one signal candle are opened within 90 minutes after its entry time (later processing is MISSED), so a cluster is complete 90 minutes after its entry_ts. Clusters are ordered by signal_ts.
Selection of clusters (no cherry-picking): UL1 uses the FIRST 15 strict clusters ordered by signal_ts; UL2 uses the FIRST 30 (cumulative, includes the UL1 clusters). A checkpoint is evaluated exactly once, on the FIRST committed state in the repository's git history in which the first N clusters (N=15 for UL1, 30 for UL2) all exist, are complete and have zero open trades (every trade closed by TP, SL or 30-bar timeout, or CENSORED relative to that commit's time (section 7A)). The evaluation instant is the commit time of that state, found by the evaluator scanning the history from the first state commit onward; the registrar's review time is irrelevant and he has no discretion over it. The trigger is detected from counts only; no R value is viewed before it holds. The cluster-timestamp set is then fixed; degraded runs do not matter because evaluation reads committed states, not runs. The evaluator (cluster building, bootstrap, jackknife, CENSORING rule, and a check that the state's created_at equals that of the first committed state; a mismatch labels the period as a defect) is a single script committed to the repo, tested on synthetic data only, and its sha256 is recorded in the repository before 5 clusters exist; any later change to it follows section 9. Later clusters are excluded from that checkpoint and its statistics even if they close earlier. Open trades outside those clusters are reported, not counted.
No outcome review before UL1; operational status (counts of signals, open, closed, missed, errors, symbol health) may be reported earlier, R values are not reported or acted upon earlier.
Statistics (fixed): cluster-mean net R by S0, S3, S3a, S3b (S1, S2 labeled, transparency only); 95% CI = percentile 2.5/97.5 of the mean of cluster means, resampling strict clusters with replacement, 20000 draws, numpy default_rng seed 20261029; P(mean>0) descriptive only. Also: trade-level mean, profit factor ("n/a" if no losses), max drawdown in R (trades ordered by exit_ts, ties by signal_ts then symbol), maximum concurrent open trades; breakdown by calendar month (month of signal_ts, UTC) and by symbol; BTC change over the window as one descriptive label (no filter).
Jackknife definitions: largest cluster = the cluster with the highest S3 cluster-mean net R, i.e. the largest contributor to the primary statistic (ties to the earlier signal_ts). Note: LONG-M1 section 7 wording ("highest S3 net R sum") is ambiguous between a trade-level sum and the cluster-mean; for U67-L the cluster-mean reading is registered; best month = the calendar month (signal_ts UTC) with the highest S3 net R sum of cluster outcomes (ties to the earlier). Each is removed once; the remaining statistic is the equal-weight mean of the remaining cluster means. If a removal leaves zero clusters, that jackknife mean is treated as <= 0.
Rate cap: if the UL1 condition (section 7, first 15 clusters complete and fully closed) is not met 120 days after the section 0e eligibility boundary, or the UL2 condition is not met 240 days after it, the observed rate is reported; no verdict. The only permitted actions are (a) continue unchanged or (b) stop the track; the choice is made once per cap, recorded before any R value is viewed, and rules, symbols and parameters are never altered.

7A. V2 CENSORING, COHORT, SENSITIVITY AND GUARD RULES (apply to U67-L only; where they differ from the wording of sections 7 and 8 - first N clusters, zero open trades, UNRESOLVED - this section governs)
1. Definitions. RESOLVED trade = closed by TP, SL or 30-bar timeout. CENSORED trade = see the censored-positions paragraph. OPEN trade = in the open set and not censored. Cluster status: OPEN (has an open trade), NONCENSORED (no open trade, at least one resolved trade), CENSORED CLUSTER (no open trade, no resolved trade, at least one censored trade).
2. Primary cohort. The first N non-censored clusters in signal_ts order (N=15 for UL1, 30 for UL2; cumulative as before). Scanning in signal_ts order, censored clusters are skipped; an OPEN cluster met before the cohort is complete blocks the checkpoint; every cohort cluster must be complete (commit time >= entry time + 90 minutes). An open trade inside a LATER cluster does not block. The checkpoint is evaluated once, on the first committed state that satisfies this.
3. Primary statistics. Cluster outcome = equal-weight mean of the cluster's RESOLVED trades' net R; censored trades carry no R. Censored clusters are excluded from primary N, minimum thresholds, bootstrap resampling and jackknife. Adjacent-merge sensitivity uses resolved trades only. Profit factor and drawdown use resolved trades. Counts of censored trades and censored clusters are always printed beside every result. Bootstrap: numpy default_rng seed 20261029, 20000 draws, as in v1.
4. Sensitivities (S3 only; fixed stress assumptions, NOT worst-case or conservative bounds, since a delisted or halted position can lose more than the assumed amount). On the SAME cohort and the SAME evaluation snapshot, censored trades receive gross R = -1 (S3 -1R sensitivity) and gross R = -2 (S3 -2R sensitivity), with the unchanged S3 cost formula, stored entry, risk and stop and bars_held = 30; only gross R changes. The -2R sensitivity is informational and never decides a verdict. All other scenarios (S0, S1, S2, S3a, S3b) apply the censoring rule identically and remain informational.
5. Mandatory limitation text in every report: fully censored clusters are excluded from the sensitivity cohort and cannot reduce the -1R/-2R sensitivities or the b2 guard; they appear only as counts.
6. Guards (they only block a pass; they never replace the primary rejection). b2: primary S3 cluster-mean > 0 and S3 -1R sensitivity <= 0. CENSORING LIMITATION: at least one fully censored cluster exists anywhere in the evaluation snapshot. At UL2 (final checkpoint): if S3 cluster-mean <= 0 -> REJECTED (unchanged); else a CENSORING LIMITATION gives the verdict NOT ESTABLISHED - CENSORING LIMITATION; else b2 gives NOT ESTABLISHED (b2); PAPER GATE PASSED is prohibited when either guard applies; otherwise the registered UL2 rule applies. At UL1 only the rejection rule is binding: a guard is printed as GUARD FLAGGED and does not convert a continuation into a rejection or a final verdict (registrar interpretation I1; to be confirmed by the designer, any other reading requires a new registration).
7. Rejection rule unchanged and on the PRIMARY statistic: S3 cluster-mean <= 0 -> REJECTED at UL1 and UL2.
8. Rate caps unchanged (section 7): if the UL1 condition is not met 120 days, or the UL2 condition 240 days, after the eligibility boundary (also when N non-censored clusters are never reached), the observed rate is reported, descriptive only, no verdict.
9. Funding-adjusted S3/S3a/S3b performance remains UNVALIDATED (flat funding proxy). The evaluator output carries this statement. The commit time is not exchange event time. U67-L jackknife results are not compared with LONG-M1.
10. Evaluator: evaluate_u67_long_v2.py (sha256 recorded outside this file); count-only trigger path; tested on synthetic data only; never run on real U67-L data at the v2 freeze.

8. DECISION RULES (decided now; S3 is the sole decision scenario)
- UL1 (15 clusters): only the rejection rule is binding. If S3 cluster-mean <= 0: REJECTED, the track is stopped; the designer may only archive it or register a new hypothesis (new registration, section 9); rules are not altered and not retested on the same observations. If S3 cluster-mean > 0: continue to UL2 with no verdict; CI and jackknife are descriptive at UL1.
- UL2 (30 clusters): REJECTED as above if S3 cluster-mean <= 0. NOT ESTABLISHED if S3 mean > 0 but CI95 lower bound <= 0, or removing the largest cluster or the best month leaves a mean <= 0. NOT ESTABLISHED is final for U67-L v2: no extension, no additional clusters, no re-evaluation; any further data requires a new registration. PAPER GATE PASSED only if S3 cluster-mean > 0 AND CI95 lower bound > 0 AND mean without the largest cluster > 0 AND mean without the best month > 0. The paper gate authorizes nothing but a further review; a real-money pilot always needs a separate explicit authorization per track and per direction.
- Tracks are judged on their own. U67-L results are never combined with U67-S, C11, LONG-M1 or any other track; opposite positions on the same symbol in different tracks (including U67-S, which shares the same 67 symbols) are not netted. Symbols are disjoint between U67-L and C11/LONG-M1 by construction; U67-L and U67-S share their symbols by design.
Decision-rule note (designer ruling 3a, 2026-10-28/29): UL1 keeps the binding REJECTED wording of the LONG-M1 registration and of U67-S; the C11 review-flag semantics are not used. The statistic is unchanged; only the consequence at UL1 is binding.
Limits of inference (stated now): clusters are treated as exchangeable in the bootstrap, but adjacent 4H clusters are dependent (the adjacent-merge sensitivity shows this); the confidence interval is conditional on that assumption. The 15-cluster rejection test and the 30-cluster test reuse the first 15 clusters, so no multiplicity correction is made. The universe is the top of one day's turnover ranking, which favors currently active names. U67-L and U67-S use the same 67 symbols in the same window, so their clusters are exposed to the same market moves; they are judged separately and never pooled.
